Best Movies of All Time (My Opinion)

My top 3 movies of all time

Man In Black

Awesome chill movie! Good for family time. Silly humor. Not a hard watch

The Silence of the Lambs

A psychological thriller about a young FBI trainee who seeks the help of a incarcerated cannibal to catch a serial killer.

Spiderman: Into the Spider-Verse

I'm a sucker for Spider-Man movies, so I needed to include one. The movie is beautifully animated and filled with easter eggs! Great watch for any age group.
